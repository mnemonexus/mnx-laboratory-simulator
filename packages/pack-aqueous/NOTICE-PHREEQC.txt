Modified data notice
====================

packages/pack-aqueous/catalogue/substances.json contains data derived from minteq.v4.dat, a
thermodynamic database distributed with PHREEQC by the U.S. Geological Survey (USGS). It is NOT
the original database. Modifications (by the MnemoNexus Laboratory project, 2026-10-26):

- Only species and phases of selected elements are kept (school and undergraduate chemistry);
  reactions involving electrons, alkali-metal ion pairs and slowly forming phases are omitted.
- Reactions are re-expressed as compositions in ion carriers with implicit protons, in a JSON
  catalogue format; log K and enthalpy values are as given in the database (kcal converted to kJ).
- Added, from the literature and NOT from the USGS database: copper, nickel and zinc ammines,
  iron(III) thiocyanate complexes, the cobalt(II) chloro complex, visible absorption spectra,
  solid colours and appearance, and reagent salts.

The original database is available in the PHREEQC distribution:
https://github.com/usgs-coupled/phreeqc3/tree/master/database (minteq.v4.dat).
Acknowledgement: data from MINTEQA2 version 4 as distributed with PHREEQC (Parkhurst and
Appelo, U.S. Geological Survey). Use of the USGS name does not imply endorsement.
